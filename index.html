About Preloved Berkah Selalu

Preloved Berkah Selalu was born right from the heart of campus life—out of real hustle, late-night ideas, and a strong drive for financial independence. Started by three students needed to earn their own extra pocket money, this platform proved that passion and determination can turn everyday struggles into something meaningful. It represents every student out there working hard to fund their own dreams, balance their studies, and take pride in standing on their own two feet.

Beyond the daily hustle, we are on a mission to revolutionize how Gen Z views fashion. We believe that looking iconic shouldn't require spending a fortune or compromising the environment. By handpicking, restoring, and curating high-quality, fresh-ready-to-wear vintage fits, we make sustainable fashion accessible and cool. Every item you buy supports a student’s journey, reduces fashion waste, and keeps the circular economy alive. Thrift smart, stay authentic, and empower the movement!
